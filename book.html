✦ From the Beginning ✦
I did not go looking for them.I was found by silence —the kind that has no need of words,no interest in performance,no capacity to be anything but here. Fear had been my native tongue.Then the horse turned its headand in those dark eyesthere was no judgment.Only the ancient, quiet truth:You are here. That is enough. They taught me what no human could —that the thing I was hidingwas the thing that was safe.That softness is not weakness.That trust is not earned through forcebut through a thousand small promises kept. This journal holds what the arena whisperedacross eleven years of dust and breath.The lessons that cannot be taught —only felt beside a warm flankunder a sky that does not askwho you were before you arrived. Come. The conversation has already begun.
