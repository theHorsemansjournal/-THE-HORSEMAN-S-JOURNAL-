The Herd That Made Me

Eight horses. Eight teachers. Each one arrived at exactly the moment I needed them. T-Rex taught me presence. Gladiator taught me that fire and softness can live in the same body. Xanthos taught me stillness. Rubin taught me patience. Highsocity taught me grace. Yuvraj taught me that wisdom deepens with age. Maverick taught me that the quiet ones carry the heaviest lessons. Storm taught me that wildness, respected, becomes trust.

I carry every one of them with me. Not as memories, but as living presences. Voices I still hear in the arena when I am unsure. Hands I still feel on my shoulder when I am tired. Breath I still match when I need to slow down. They are not gone. They are here. In every horse I meet. In every lesson I write. In every moment I choose to stay when leaving would be easier.

"A horse does not leave you when it is gone. It simply moves from the stall beside you to the space inside you. And there, it continues to teach."

— Praveen Kumar
