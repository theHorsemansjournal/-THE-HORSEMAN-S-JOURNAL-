The Unspoken Finds Its Shape

These eleven verses are fragments of a life lived honestly — in grief, in longing, in the slow excavation of a self buried under years of performance. They are not easy to read. They were not easy to write. But they are true. And sometimes, truth is the only thing worth offering.

✨ May you find your own words for the silences that have been holding you. ✨

— Praveen Kumar
